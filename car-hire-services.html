Your Journey, Our Priority

Reliable, comfortable transportation is the backbone of any exceptional East African tourism experience. Whether you're embarking on a multi-day safari, need airport transfers, or prefer the freedom of self-drive exploration, our premium car hire services ensure you travel in style and safety.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our car hire tourism services

What documents do I need for self-drive car hire?

+
For self-drive car hire, you'll need a valid driver's license (international driving permit recommended), passport, and a credit card for the security deposit. The minimum age is 23 years with at least 2 years of driving experience.

Is insurance included in the rental price?

+
Yes, all our rentals include comprehensive insurance covering third-party liability, vehicle damage, and theft. However, a security deposit is required, which is fully refundable upon return of the vehicle in good condition.

Can I drive the vehicle anywhere in East Africa?

+
Our vehicles can be driven throughout Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, and Rwanda. Cross-border travel is permitted with prior arrangement. Some restrictions apply to certain remote areas for safety reasons—our team will advise you on route planning.

What is the fuel policy?

+
For self-drive rentals, vehicles are provided with a full tank and should be returned full. For chauffeur-driven services, fuel is included in the daily rate. We can also provide fuel cost estimates for your planned tourism itinerary.

What happens if the vehicle breaks down?

+
We provide 24/7 roadside assistance across East Africa. In case of breakdown, contact our emergency line immediately. We'll dispatch a replacement vehicle or arrange repairs as quickly as possible, minimizing disruption to your tourism plans.

Can I modify my booking or extend the rental period?

+
Yes, bookings can be modified or extended subject to vehicle availability. We recommend contacting us at least 24 hours in advance for changes. Extension rates will apply based on current pricing at the time of extension.
