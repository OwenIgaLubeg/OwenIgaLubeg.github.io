Coffee Tourism in Uganda

Discover Uganda's Coffee Heritage

Uganda is Africa's largest coffee producer and the birthplace of Robusta coffee. Our coffee tourism experiences invite you to explore the lush plantations where world-class coffee is grown, processed, and roasted.

From the fertile slopes of Mount Elgon to the rolling hills of Central Uganda, visit working coffee farms, learn about the production process from cherry to cup, and taste freshly roasted Ugandan coffee at its source.

Join our exclusive Coffee Investment Club and become part of Uganda's growing coffee industry while enjoying exclusive tours, tastings, and investment opportunities.
